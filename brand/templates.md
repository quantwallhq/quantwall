## Words we do not use

course, teach, teaching, lesson, chapter, session, subject, student, lecture, syllabus. Say "this channel", "video", "section", "part", "topic".

## Video rules

- No dollar figures or return percentages as promises; no "bot", "secret" or "guaranteed".
- A title must still be true if every strategy lost money.
- Disclaimer spoken in the first 30 seconds, full text in the description, nothing burned in across the whole video.
- Captions are uploaded as an .srt, not burned in.
